      📚 Past commissions

      📜 House rules
      • Two main spins. Each spin picks a category first, then rolls the details inside that category. The detail rolls don't use up a spin.
      • Same category twice is fine. Two Accessory results can happen. The exact same finished challenge in both slots is redrawn automatically.
      • The commission comes first. If the roulette conflicts with what the customer asked for, the customer wins. Reroll that slot and say why.
      • Valid reroll reasons: exact duplicate, conflicts with the commission, not unlocked or incompatible, or another genuinely impossible situation (add a note). "I don't like how it looks" isn't a valid reason.
      • Accessories: at least 3 pieces, arranged on purpose. Lace trim and trim (including piping) count as 3 distinct decorated areas.
      • Fabric: it must have a meaningful visible presence. Burlap is never rolled.
      • Pattern Piece: a Random Pattern is binding unless it's impossible, and used patterns can come up again. With Choose Unused, you pick any unlocked pattern you've never used. If none are left, it switches to Random Pattern. The app can't tell which pieces fit together, so reroll a genuinely incompatible result.
      • The app doesn't check Quality, Gothic, Risqué, percentages, prices or compatibility. Check those yourself in the game.
